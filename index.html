Reservation Policy and Terms and Conditions

In addition to the KTV room packages, Chop House Resto Bar x Cafe follows reservation policies regarding down payment, cancellation, refund, rescheduling, and customer responsibilities.

Reservation Requirements

Customers are required to provide accurate reservation details, valid ID, and proof of payment before their reservation can be approved by the staff or admin.

Down Payment Policy

The establishment follows a down payment policy where the PHP 1,000 reservation fee is non-refundable, while the PHP 3,000 reservation fee is refundable upon verification and approval by the management or authorized staff.

Cancellation and Rescheduling

Customers who wish to cancel or change their reservation must inform the establishment at least 1 day before the scheduled reservation time. Change of date is allowed depending on the availability of the selected KTV room or VIP lounge.

Approval and No-Show Policy

Rescheduling, cancellation, and refund requests are still subject to staff or admin approval. If a customer fails to arrive or does not notify the establishment, the reservation may be marked as cancelled or no-show, and the room or lounge may be made available to other customers.

House Rules

The establishment prohibits outside food and drinks inside the KTV room and VIP lounges. By submitting a reservation, customers agree to follow the cancellation, refund, rescheduling, and house rules of Chop House Resto Bar x Cafe.
